# Third-party notices

runex's native addon (`runex.<platform>.node`, published in the `@runex-js/binding-<platform>`
packages) statically links **Rune** and its crates, and embeds what they bundle: the **Inter**
and **IBM Plex Mono** typefaces, and **OpenCode**'s design tokens and icons. Their notices follow.

The repository carries more of Rune than the addon does: `examples/` are ports of Rune's examples
and include their assets, `src/gen` and `ts/gen` (and the published `.d.ts` files) carry Rune's
API documentation, and `patches/` modify Rune. All of it is under Rune's MIT license below; the
example fonts keep their OFL texts beside them.

The addon also links about 200 to 300 Rust crates from crates.io, depending on the platform
(wgpu, winit, napi-rs and their dependencies). `cargo tree -e normal --target <triple>` lists
them, and each crate's license text ships in its source on crates.io. Most are MIT and/or
Apache-2.0; the rest are Unicode-3.0, Zlib, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, BSL-1.0
(`clipboard-win` and `error-code`, Windows only) and MPL-2.0. The MPL-2.0 crates are
`symphonia` 0.5 (`symphonia-core`, `-metadata`, `-format-ogg`, `-format-mkv`, `-codec-vorbis`,
`-utils-xiph`) and `opuscule` 0.2, which only the macOS addon includes, through Rune's `ogg-audio`
feature. They are used unmodified, and their source is at https://crates.io/crates/symphonia and
https://crates.io/crates/opuscule.
